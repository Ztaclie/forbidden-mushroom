[h1]FORBIDDEN MUSHROOM[/h1]
[i]Grow meat. Eat the forbidden. Every power has a price.[/i]

One strange organism that feeds a whole colony: [b]meat without animals[/b], [b]16 caps[/b] that supercharge your pawns, and [b]traps and weapons that take enemies alive[/b]. Every cap has a downside, and research lets you refine it away.

[b]No DLC needed. No other mods. Safe to add to an existing save. RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7.[/b]
[hr][/hr]

[h2]THE MEAT SHROOM[/h2]
Grows in soil or hydroponics, in the dark, with no research. Harvest 10 meat shroom flesh every 7 days.
[list]
[*] A raw meat that every meat recipe accepts: meals, kibble, pemmican
[*] Meals made with it give a small mood boost. Animals eat it. Traders stock and buy it.
[/list]

[h2]MYCELIUM SNARE[/h2]
A hidden one-tile trap (15 flesh, no research). It roots the victim in mycelium: they go down for capture and starve much faster. Lay them side by side like caltrops.

[h2]REMOVE THE PRICE[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Refined caps[/b]: research a group, then steep a cap in shroom broth at a stove (1 cap + 5 flesh). Same benefit, no downside. The forbidden caps lose some raw power too.
[*] [b]Packages[/b]: bundle a group's caps into one leaf-wrapped package that applies every effect in a single action. Combat, recovery, mind and worker packages, each with a refined version.
[/list]
